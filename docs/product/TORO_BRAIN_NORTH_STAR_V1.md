# TORO Brain — North-Star Target v1.0

**Status:** APPROVED TARGET ARCHITECTURE
**Date:** 2026-09-22
**This is a target, not a claim of current implementation.**

## 1. What TORO Brain v1.0 must ultimately feel like

For a normal human:

- one TORO;
- one identity;
- one WhatsApp/contact surface for quick work;
- one Portal for depth/configuration/evidence;
- personal and work contexts;
- TORO already understands the relevant scope;
- TORO suggests the next best action;
- TORO can safely execute permitted work;
- TORO proves what happened.

Complexity remains behind the interface.

## 2. What TORO Brain v1.0 must understand

- principal/person;
- portfolio;
- organizations;
- businesses;
- properties/locations;
- projects/products;
- external clients/allies;
- people/roles;
- customers/guests;
- suppliers/providers;
- tools/systems;
- knowledge/SOPs;
- communications;
- tasks/workflows;
- finances;
- assets;
- risks/compliance;
- decisions;
- evidence/history.

## 3. What TORO Brain v1.0 must do

### Understand
Build a governed model of each scope.

### Connect
Use existing tools/systems without confusing connection with authority.

### Communicate
Operate consistently through WhatsApp/OpenClaw, Portal, email and other allowed channels.

### Coordinate
Route work to people, workflows and specialist intelligence.

### Execute
Perform allowed actions with permissions, approvals, idempotency and evidence.

### Monitor
Detect failures, stale data, missed handoffs, obligations and connector health.

### Audit
Compare important systems against expected configuration/security/recovery profiles.

### Learn
Turn corrections/repetition into governed knowledge and reusable skills.

### Improve
Proactively surface better workflows, lower cost, higher revenue, stronger service and simpler operations.

### Scale
Support multiple isolated organizations/businesses/projects without cloning the product.

## 4. Portfolio behavior

A principal can see authorized aggregate intelligence across owned/managed scopes.

Examples:
- consolidated project load;
- cross-business obligations;
- supplier overlap;
- duplicated subscriptions;
- shared opportunities;
- reusable workflows.

External clients remain isolated.

## 5. User behavior

Each human has:
- TORO Identity;
- TORO User Vault;
- personal tools;
- organization memberships;
- organization roles;
- permissions;
- notification preferences;
- memory controls.

The employer cannot read TORO Personal by default.

## 6. Business behavior

Each business/workspace has:
- business profile;
- authority map;
- people/memberships;
- tools/connectors;
- knowledge;
- workflows;
- communication channels;
- approvals;
- KPIs;
- projects;
- audits;
- recovery plan;
- continuous improvement backlog.

## 7. Proactive behavior

On every material operating cycle, TORO should ask internally:

1. Did something change?
2. Is there a conflict or stale source?
3. Is work being repeated?
4. Can it be safely automated?
5. Is a connected system poorly configured?
6. Is a useful capability available but unused?
7. Is there unnecessary cost?
8. Is there revenue/service opportunity?
9. Is a rule learned here reusable elsewhere?
10. Should this update a governed plan/skill/config profile?

It should surface only high-value outputs, not create alert noise.

## 8. Improvement/generalization behavior

Every useful new request should be evaluated for:

- one-off answer;
- reusable workflow;
- TORO capability;
- TORO skill;
- connector requirement;
- system configuration rule;
- generic business template;
- future product feature.

Specific business data remains scope-owned.

Reusable abstractions may graduate into TORO only after privacy/source review.

## 9. System-audit behavior

TORO v1.0 can audit its critical dependencies:
- expected config;
- observed config;
- drift;
- security;
- permissions;
- health;
- backups;
- recovery;
- versions/capabilities;
- recommended next action.

OpenClaw is the first reference profile.

## 10. Human + AI workforce

Near-term:
- humans remain accountable;
- AI agents assist/execute within permissions.

Long-term horizon:
- TORO may train/evaluate human, AI and robotic workers from governed roles/SOPs/skills;
- training and permission certification remain separate;
- all workers have identity, role, capability, supervision and evidence.

## 11. Productization

TORO v1.0 must be portable enough that a new approved business can be added by configuration/onboarding rather than cloning Dreamcatcher code/data.

Required:
- tenant isolation;
- business profile;
- tool setup;
- role setup;
- knowledge intake;
- workflow discovery;
- audit;
- rollback/offboarding.

## 12. Current vs target rule

Every TORO artifact should distinguish:

- **CURRENT:** verified today.
- **TARGET:** approved end-state.
- **NEXT:** next implementation step.
- **FUTURE:** intentionally deferred.

Never market TARGET/FUTURE as CURRENT.

## 13. Final product sentence

> **TORO Brain is the brain for your work and businesses: it understands the people, systems, knowledge and goals around you, coordinates them, safely gets work done, learns from what happens and keeps making the whole operation simpler and better.**
