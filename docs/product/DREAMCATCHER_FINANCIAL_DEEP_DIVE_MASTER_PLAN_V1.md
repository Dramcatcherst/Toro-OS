# Dreamcatcher Financial Deep-Dive — Master Plan v1

**Status:** CANONICAL ANALYSIS PLAN  
**Owner directive:** 2026-09-30  
**Owner:** TORO Finance + TORO Revenue + Dreamcatcher Operations  
**Purpose:** build a complete, evidence-backed financial model of Dreamcatcher before making coordinated pricing, cost, supplier, staffing, channel or package changes.

---

## 1. Operating principle

Do not make broad pricing/cost changes from isolated estimates.

Sequence:

1. **Collect**
2. **Reconcile**
3. **Classify**
4. **Model**
5. **Compare**
6. **Simulate**
7. **Approve**
8. **Execute coordinated change day**
9. **Verify**
10. **Monitor**

The objective is not simply to reduce expenses. It is to improve **sustainable profit, cash generation, contribution per occupied room-night and resilience**, while protecting guest experience and revenue.

---

## 2. Core questions this program must answer

### Hotel-level
- Is Dreamcatcher truly profitable after separating operating, financing, tax, capital and owner/non-operating items?
- What is the monthly cash break-even?
- What is the monthly operating break-even?
- What is the sustainable break-even including replacement reserves?
- What occupancy is required to break even at different ADRs?
- What ADR is required to break even at different occupancy levels?
- Which months generate or destroy profit?
- Which costs are structurally too high?
- Which expenses can be reduced without hurting service or revenue?
- Which expenses should be increased because they generate a positive return?

### Room-level
- What does each room actually cost when occupied?
- What is each room's cash floor?
- What is each room's sustainable floor?
- What is the minimum direct price?
- What is the minimum OTA price?
- What happens with 1, 2, 3, 4+ guests?
- What happens when breakfast is included?
- Which rooms generate the most contribution per occupied night?
- Which rooms consume disproportionate maintenance/utilities?
- Which rooms are underpriced or overpriced relative to real economics?

### Channel-level
- Direct vs Booking vs Expedia vs agency contribution.
- Commission leakage.
- Payment-processing cost.
- Cancellation/no-show effect.
- Discount stacking.
- Net ADR by channel.
- Cost of acquisition per reservation.
- Whether a higher OTA ADR actually produces less contribution than a lower direct ADR.

### Food / breakfast
- Real breakfast cost per person served.
- $15 retail breakfast contribution.
- $30 room-rate uplift for 2 people vs true incremental cost.
- Included but not consumed economics.
- Extra breakfast economics.
- Supplier settlement economics.
- Whether breakfast improves ADR/conversion/LOS/direct share enough to justify bundling.

### Labor
- Payroll as % revenue.
- Cost by department.
- Housekeeping cost per occupied room-night / turnover.
- Reception cost per occupied room / reservation.
- Maintenance fixed vs reactive/project work.
- Overtime / replacement / turnover cost.
- Staffing thresholds by occupancy.

### Utilities / property costs
- Electricity base load vs occupancy-driven usage.
- Water base load vs occupancy-driven usage.
- Internet / TV / telecom.
- Pools / jacuzzis.
- Laundry.
- Cleaning supplies.
- Waste / leaks / abnormal spikes.
- Unit cost by building/property where meters allow.

### Maintenance / capex
- Routine maintenance vs project/capex.
- Preventive vs corrective spend.
- Vehicle repairs vs hotel repairs.
- Replacement reserve per room.
- Which assets cause recurring cost.
- Whether preventive investment reduces break-even long-term.

### Software / subscriptions
- Every recurring subscription.
- Monthly/annual cost.
- Owner.
- Purpose.
- Actual usage.
- Duplicate tools.
- Replace / consolidate / cancel candidates.
- Cost per reservation / room / employee where relevant.

### Finance / banking
- Banking fees.
- Card acquiring fees.
- FX losses.
- Financing interest.
- Loans separated from operations.
- Processor settlement/retention leakage.
- Reconciliation completeness.

### Tax / accounting
- IVA separated from revenue.
- Tax expense separated from operating cost.
- Supported vs unsupported expenses.
- Missing invoices/comprobantes.
- Personal / shareholder items separated from operating economics.
- Correct cost-center allocation.

## 3. Financial layers

TORO Finance must maintain distinct views:

### A. Revenue
- gross guest payment;
- taxes/pass-throughs;
- net hotel revenue.

### B. Variable / marginal cost
- per reservation;
- per stay;
- per occupied night;
- per guest;
- per sale/channel.

### C. Step-fixed cost
Costs that change when occupancy/staffing crosses thresholds.

### D. Fixed operating cost
Costs that exist regardless of current occupancy.

### E. Replacement / sustainability reserve
Normal long-term asset wear.

### F. Capex
Expansion / major renovation / long-life assets.

### G. Financing
Interest, loan fees, principal separated.

### H. Tax
Income tax and statutory charges separated from operating economics.

### I. Owner / non-operating
Shareholder distributions, personal expenses, owner-paid items pending treatment, etc.

## 5. Change-day operating model

No broad execution before analysis is decision-ready.

Create a future **FINANCIAL OPTIMIZATION CHANGE DAY** only after:

- source reconciliation is complete enough;
- proposed changes are costed;
- expected benefit is quantified;
- operational dependencies are known;
- guest impact is reviewed;
- rollback exists;
- owners/approvers are assigned.

Possible change-day actions:
- rate plan changes;
- breakfast package repricing;
- direct/OTA pricing floors;
- promotion limits;
- supplier renegotiation;
- subscription cancellations;
- staffing/schedule changes;
- utility controls;
- process changes;
- accounting classification fixes;
- dashboard/alert activation.

Each change requires:
- expected monthly impact;
- implementation owner;
- evidence;
- rollback;
- verification date.

## 6. Master discovery questionnaire

These questions should be resolved progressively. Do not block all analysis waiting for every answer.

### Breakfast / food
1. Is the current public breakfast price always USD 15 per person?
2. Is tax included in the USD 15?
3. What does the supplier charge the hotel per breakfast?
4. Is supplier payment based on planned breakfasts or actually consumed breakfasts?
5. Are children charged the same?
6. Are staff/house breakfasts charged differently?
7. Are drinks/coffee included in supplier cost?
8. Are there multiple breakfast menus with different costs?
9. Does the hotel provide ingredients/supplies separately?
10. Does the supplier invoice weekly or monthly?

### Housekeeping / laundry
11. Is housekeeping payroll mostly fixed salary, per shift or per room?
12. How many rooms can one housekeeper clean per normal shift?
13. Is laundry internal or external?
14. What is the actual linen cycle per checkout?
15. How often are towels changed on multi-night stays?
16. Do villas require materially different cleaning time?
17. Are there deep-cleaning costs not captured in normal housekeeping?

### Utilities
18. Which buildings have separate electric meters?
19. Which have separate water meters?
20. Are pools/jacuzzis on dedicated circuits/meters?
21. What A/C models/capacities are in each room?
22. Are there known high-consumption rooms?
23. What is the hot-water system by building?
24. Are there leaks/base-load issues already known?

### Labor
25. Which salaries should be treated as normal operating payroll vs owner/management compensation?
26. What staff increases are triggered by occupancy?
27. Are overtime/extra shifts tracked consistently?
28. Which functions could be scheduled more efficiently without reducing service?

### Maintenance
29. Which 2026 repairs were expansion/capital projects vs normal operating repairs?
30. Which vehicle costs are unrelated to room operations?
31. Which recurring repairs are preventable?
32. What equipment has high failure frequency?

### Channels / payment
33. Exact Booking commission by rate/plan?
34. Expedia commission?
35. Direct card acquiring fee by card/network?
36. Are OTA commissions calculated on tax-inclusive or tax-exclusive amounts?
37. Are there promotion stacking cases?
38. Are there virtual-card/FX fees?
39. What percentage of bookings are truly direct?

### Pricing
40. What are the current active rate plans in Kross?
41. Which include breakfast?
42. What is the room-only vs breakfast difference for each plan?
43. Are there floor prices already configured?
44. Which promotions can override floors?
45. Are villas priced by unit or guest count?

### Accounting / evidence
46. Are all 2026 supplier invoices being entered into Alegra?
47. Which expense classes frequently arrive only by WhatsApp/email?
48. Which suppliers lack electronic invoices?
49. Are all card/bank settlements reconciled?
50. Which categories contain personal/shareholder items?

### Software / subscriptions
51. List every hotel subscription.
52. Which are paid monthly vs annually?
53. Which tools overlap?
54. Which tools are unused or low-value?
55. Which subscriptions are personal but used by hotel operations?

### Capex / expansion
56. Which 2026 expenditures belong to the hotel expansion?
57. Which improvements should be capitalized rather than treated as monthly operating cost?
58. What replacement reserve should be assigned to A/C, TVs, projectors, locks, furniture and linens?

## 8. Standing optimization objective

TORO must continually search for ways to lower break-even while protecting or increasing revenue and guest value.

Prioritize opportunities by:

```text
Net monthly benefit
× confidence
× reversibility
÷ implementation effort / risk
```

Categories:
- REMOVE WASTE
- REDUCE COST
- SHIFT FIXED → VARIABLE
- INCREASE DIRECT SHARE
- IMPROVE PRICE / MIX
- IMPROVE PRODUCTIVITY
- RENEGOTIATE
- AUTOMATE
- RECLASSIFY / CLEAN DATA
- INVEST TO SAVE

Do not treat every lower cost as an improvement. Costs that protect conversion, service, reviews, asset life or revenue may be worth increasing.

## 9. Acceptance criteria

This financial deep-dive is decision-ready when:
- revenue is reconciled;
- at least 90% of material operating expenses are classified;
- non-operating items are separated;
- room-night / occupancy data is reconciled;
- channel fees are known;
- breakfast economics are known;
- payroll is mapped;
- utilities are split into base vs usage-driven where possible;
- monthly break-even has confidence bounds;
- room-level floors exist;
- at least 3 months back-test successfully against accounting results;
- proposed changes have expected impact and rollback.
