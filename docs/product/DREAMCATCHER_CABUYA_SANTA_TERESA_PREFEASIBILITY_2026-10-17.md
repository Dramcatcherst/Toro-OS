### Santa Teresa

The source ICT certificate grants a 2021 tourism declaration for hotel lodging. It does not prove a Tourism Contract, expansion approval, liquor license, restaurant, rooftop or nightclub authorization. It expressly requires prior communication/approval for material changes in infrastructure, rooms and complementary services.

Room 11 is currently represented as an active sellable accommodation. Casa/Room 13 is a private street-side space and does not belong to Dreamcatcher Villa. A conversion must preserve that distinction and compare lost lodging revenue against incremental food/beverage or entertainment margin.

## 4. Product direction

### Cabuya

Study a low-density boutique product based on nature, wellbeing, small retreats and a disciplined food-and-beverage offer. An 8–12 key/villa first-stage model is a scenario for prefeasibility only; site capacity and program remain unknown until Gate 0 and technical studies pass.

Do not transfer Santa Teresa ADR or premium comparables directly to Cabuya. Build demand, rate, occupancy and RevPAR scenarios from Cabuya/Montezuma evidence and actual seasonal distribution data.

### Santa Teresa

Evaluate in separate cost/revenue packages:

1. old-room renovation;
2. a street-facing local/lounge associated with Casa 13;
3. a rooftop restaurant;
4. the owner-requested room 11 + 13 bar/nightclub scenario as a high-risk alternative.

Preferred sequence: room product first; street-facing low-intensity lounge second; rooftop only after structure/egress; nightclub only if land use, noise, parking, safety and displaced-revenue tests pass. Do not consume room 11 in the base case.

## 5. Regulatory gates

### Land, water and design

1. authenticated registry literal, cadastral plan, coordinates, access and encumbrances;
2. ZMT/coastal-plan overlay and exact municipal land use;
3. water availability or concession and wastewater solution;
4. topography, geotechnics, drainage, parking and accessibility;
5. SETENA route based on area, earth movement and environmental fragility;
6. CFIA/APC, municipal construction permit, INS and inspections.

Cóbano publishes the applicable ZMT/coastal planning context: <https://www.municobano.go.cr/index.php/es/gestion-municipal/servicios-tributarios/zona-maritimo-terrestre/departamento-zmt>.

### Rooftop, restaurant and bar

Validate each exact activity and change of use, including structure, seismic/wind loads, occupancy, evacuation, fire systems, GLP/kitchen, grease and wastewater, accessibility, noise confinement, parking, sanitary permit, municipal license and liquor category/distances.

The existing hotel license/declaration does not automatically extend to an independent street business or nightclub.

### ICT and incentives

Tourism Declaration and Tourism Contract are separate. ICT lists declaration, approved economic study, approved construction/as-built plans and legal/technical requirements for a Tourism Contract: <https://ict.go.cr/es/servicios-institucionales/contrato-turistico.html>.

Do not acquire or import on an assumed exemption. Confirm the exact approved activity, same legal holder, integration of complementary services and purchase plan first.

## 6. Financing and tokenization

The most defensible first financing path is a conventional bankable package. BCR Pyme Acción Turismo publicly includes lodging/gastronomy and sustainable construction, remodeling, energy, water, waste and climate resilience, but does not constitute approval or publish project-specific amount/rate/term: <https://sbdcr.com/programa-bcr-pyme-accion-turismo-fortalecera-la-adaptacion-climatica-del-sector-turistico/>.

Prepare entity/beneficial-owner documents, Mipyme evidence, financial statements, debt, equity contribution, guarantees, title, permits, line-item budget and cash flow. Compare written indications from at least two accredited operators before selecting financing.

No token, membership, NFT or fractional product may be offered, advertised or funded. The legal analysis must classify the economic right by substance, public/private offering perimeter, issuer/SPV/trust, jurisdictions, investor profile, transferability, custody/platform, AML/KYC, tax, governance, bank subordination and registry effect. A token does not itself transfer real-estate title.

## 7. Cost boundary

The Costa Rican fiscal replacement-cost manual is a benchmark, not a turnkey quote. Current references include hotel typologies around CRC 690,000–1,080,000/m2 and restaurant typologies around CRC 495,000–1,050,000/m2: <https://www.hacienda.go.cr/docs/ManualdeValoresBaseUnitariosporTipologiaConstructiva.pdf>.

Any project budget must separately price land/site risk, FF&E, kitchen, pool/decks, access/parking, power, water, wastewater, professional studies, permits, financing, opening capital and contingency. Do not calculate a total until measured areas and infrastructure scopes exist.
