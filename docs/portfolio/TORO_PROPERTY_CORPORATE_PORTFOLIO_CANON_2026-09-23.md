## 1. Governance rule

TORO must never use one label interchangeably for a legal entity, a physical property, a brand, a debt, a project, a concession/right or a managed external asset.

`property / right -> legal holder or claimant -> legal entity/person -> source -> date -> confidence -> unresolved gap`

A folder name, payer, project nickname, shareholder relationship or accounting label does not prove title.
